TV Games in scratch might just be possible! - By @Yiosgio

Wait what? TV support in scratch? how does that work? let's explain how this discovery might help with compatibility. Well actually it was tested on a single device. Here's the exact informations i tested on.

OS: VseeBox (Android TV) Browser: Firefox on Android The Controls below may work for other devices but i am not sure.

Left/right/up/down - Output: Left/right/up/down Arrow keys OK - Output: Enter Numberpads (1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,0), = Numberpads. They're the same as the input.

- ALL OF THE FOLLOWING were tested on Scratch Which has many performance issues, The ones that crashed take TOO long to load - Anyway here are projects that are compatiable as of now. (And dont crash)

Boxing Minion Game: Parallax Universes Collide - Because it only needs left/right/up/down to function.

Super Minion Clicker/Tree Minion Clicker - Because they only need clicking to function and the tv i am using offers a mouse mode. Cant use movement at the same time though

Mini-Game Minions - Same reason as Boxing Minion Game: Parallax Universes Collide

Benny OST 2 - despite its large music size, the game ran fine. I guess the request of Megabytes are maybe the crashes?

Minion Plate - a new device is added, All it really does is turn off mouse and use the controller mode that is used for CO-Op but for single-player.

Benny Maker 2 - I cut down music size by a lot. Ran with turbowarp. Although very slow, having lots of clones lags the tv.
