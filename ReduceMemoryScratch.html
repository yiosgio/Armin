Reduce storage in your scratch projects - By @Yiosgio

What if you wanted to share your scratch project with a friend and you get a "This file is too big" prompt? In this article i will show you how to optimize your scratch game so you can share that project with your friends.

Music/Sounds (the biggest of any scratch project)

Mono to stereo, using WAV format takes WAY more space than mp4

Stick to widely supported audio formats. You may think this will make no effect but it CAN, Curtain audio tagged files don't work, don't use them or (if needed) use different audio tags, you will see what i mean when you try to play your scratch games outside of PC.

Cropping Music Length, if a song repeats its instruments over and over in the same sound, you can only have just one, Can make drastic changes to your storage.

Remove/Delete unused Sounds, Check the code to see if the sound is even being played.

And still if you can't fit it into the limit, Remove all music or have only 1 music.

Costumes (Small Improvements)

SVG to bitmap since its put to one layer, it can ruin graphics, Best used for pixel-art styles.

(SVG Only) make the costume smaller then enlarge it with sprite size, Its less data to store.

Code/Scripts (Not a lot)

Removed unused scripts like Custom blocks, broadcasts, You may also consider removing unused sprites.
